# Educator-directory listing

## Title

MeloLingua CEFR-Graded Multilingual Stories Dataset

## Resource type

Dataset; teaching material; graded reading; reading-comprehension activity; vocabulary resource

## Description

A reusable collection of 118 public A1–B2 graded stories in German, Spanish, French, Italian, Korean, and Russian. Every record includes target-language text, sentence-aligned English translations, contextual vocabulary, comprehension questions, sentence-building exercises, teaching metadata, provenance, and a link to the learner-facing story on MeloLingua.

## Audience

- Language teachers and tutors
- Adult and secondary language learners
- Curriculum and instructional designers
- Applied-linguistics and educational-technology researchers
- Developers of non-commercial educational prototypes

## Education levels

CEFR A1, A2, B1, and B2

## Languages

German, Spanish, French, Italian, Korean, and Russian, with English translations.
